Part 2: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This policy explains what Mark PDF does with information, and what it deliberately does not do. Zest Mavericks is the controller of the personal data described here, and the data fiduciary for the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Your documents

Documents you open, scans you take, signatures you draw, watermarks you add and passwords you set are stored on your device and in whatever storage you choose through the iOS Files system, such as iCloud Drive if you save there. We do not run an account system, we have no server that holds your files, and we cannot see them.

The one exception is AI summarisation, described next. Everything else, including scanning, merging, splitting, compressing, extracting images, watermarking, signing and password protection, happens on your device.

2. AI summarisation

When you ask for a summary, the text of the document you selected leaves your device and is sent to Apple Inc., which generates the summary and returns it. This happens only when you start it, only for the document you chose, and only while you have an active Pro subscription.

That text is not used to train any model, and the provider retains it only for 30 Days. We do not store the document, the summary, or a log of the content on our own systems.

A document can contain personal or confidential information, so treat this feature as a decision to share that document with a third party processor, and use your judgement about which documents you summarise.

3. Converting a web page

The App requests the page directly from your device using the address you type. The site you visit will see that request in the same way it would see a browser visit. We do not proxy it and we do not keep a record of the addresses you convert.

4. Diagnostics and analytics

We use Google Firebase to collect crash reports, performance data and other diagnostics when the App misbehaves. This can include device model and manufacturer, operating system version, app version, general device settings, and information about which screens and features were in use around the time of a problem.

This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We use it to find bugs, to see whether new features work, and to improve the App. It never includes the contents of your documents.

Google acts as our processor for this and its own privacy terms apply to the data it handles on our behalf.

5. Purchases

Apple handles every payment. We never see or store your card details, billing address or Apple Account credentials. We receive only what we need to unlock Pro features, which is whether a valid subscription exists on the device, together with anonymous aggregate sales reports from App Store Connect.

6. Permissions the App asks for

  • Photos and Files: only when you choose to import or export something, and only for the items you pick.

You can withdraw any of these in the iOS Settings app at any time. Some features stop working without them.
