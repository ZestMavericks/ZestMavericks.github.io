About Zest Mavericks

Zest Mavericks is a two person studio in Bangalore, India, building absurdly intuitive apps for iPhone. It makes MarkPDF, a PDF editor and scanner, and TopDrawer, a private vault for everything you save.

MarkPDF

MarkPDF is a PDF editor and scanner for iPhone. Scan paper into PDFs, sign, merge, split and lock documents, delete pages, and summarise long PDFs with AI, from one calm, uncluttered app.

TopDrawer

TopDrawer is a private vault for iPhone. Save text, links, screenshots, PDFs and files from anywhere and find any of them with one search, including the words inside your screenshots. Everything stays on the device: there's no account and no sync.
