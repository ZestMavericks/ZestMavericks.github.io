MarkPDF vs Preview: do you need a PDF app on iPhone?

Checked 1 October 2026

Maybe not. Since iOS 26, the free Preview app on your iPhone scans, signs, marks up, combines PDFs, edits pages and adds passwords. MarkPDF is for people who live in PDFs: it adds AI summaries, compression, editing the existing text, watermarks and image extraction, all from one home screen.

What Preview does for free

  • Scan paper into a PDF with the camera.
  • Mark up, fill in forms and sign.
  • Combine PDFs into one.
  • Add, delete, rotate, move and crop pages.
  • Lock a PDF with a password.

For an occasional form or a signature, that's everything you need, and it's already on your phone.

What MarkPDF adds

  • AI summaries: the shape of a long document in seconds, before you read it.
  • Compression, for files too big to email or upload.
  • Editing the existing text, not just adding notes on top.
  • Watermarks and image extraction.
  • Web page to PDF.
  • One home screen with every tool on it, so you don't hunt through menus.

Scanning is free in MarkPDF. Everything else is part of MarkPDF Pro.

Our honest advice

Try Preview first. If you find yourself wanting a summary of a 60-page report, squeezing a file under an upload limit, or fixing a typo in a PDF someone sent you, that's when MarkPDF earns its place.

How we checked

Locking and page editing come from Apple's iPhone User Guide for iOS 26 (locking PDFs, editing pages); scanning, signing and combining from AppleInsider's guide to Preview in iOS 26. Checked on 1 October 2026. Compression, text editing and AI summaries aren't in Apple's guide for Preview on iPhone. See also MarkPDF vs PDF Expert vs Adobe Acrobat.

MarkPDF is free on the App Store for iPhone.
