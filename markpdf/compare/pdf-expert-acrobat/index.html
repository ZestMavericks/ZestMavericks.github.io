MarkPDF vs PDF Expert vs Adobe Acrobat on iPhone

Checked 1 October 2026

PDF Expert and Adobe Acrobat let you read, mark up and sign for free, and charge for merging, compressing, passwords, editing text and AI. MarkPDF gives away scanning and puts everything else in MarkPDF Pro, at $39.99 a year: the lowest yearly price of the three, with AI summaries included. And since iOS 26, Apple's free Preview app signs, combines, edits pages and adds passwords too.

Which one fits you

  • Preview, if you sign, mark up, combine or lock the odd PDF. It's free and already on your iPhone, so start there.
  • PDF Expert, if you work on the same documents across iPhone, iPad and Mac. Its cross-device plan is built for that.
  • Adobe Acrobat, if your work already runs on Adobe's cloud and shared PDF reviews.
  • MarkPDF, if you want the whole toolset on your iPhone for the lowest yearly price here, AI summaries included, in an app with one calm home screen instead of a toolbar of everything.
