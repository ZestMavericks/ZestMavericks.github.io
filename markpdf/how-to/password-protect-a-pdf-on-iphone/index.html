How to password-protect a PDF on iPhone

Updated 1 October 2026

Open MarkPDF, tap Lock PDF, choose the document, type a password and tap Lock PDF. The PDF then asks for that password before it opens, and Remove Lock takes the password off again.

Step by step

  1. Open MarkPDF. On the home screen, under Edit, tap Lock PDF.
  2. Choose the PDF you want to protect.
  3. Type a password in the Lock Document box.
  4. Tap Lock PDF.

Removing the password

On the home screen, under Edit, tap Remove Lock, choose the locked PDF and enter its password.

Choosing the password

  • Use something long that you'll remember, or save it in the Passwords app on your iPhone.
  • Share the password separately from the file, for example by message rather than in the same email.

MarkPDF is free on the App Store for iPhone.
