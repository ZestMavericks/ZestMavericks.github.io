How to scan documents to PDF on iPhone

Updated 1 October 2026

Open MarkPDF, tap Scan Document, then Scan Documents, and hold your iPhone over the page. MarkPDF finds the edges of the page and captures it; scan every page, tap Save, and they're saved together as a PDF.

Step by step

  1. Open MarkPDF. On the home screen, under Create, tap Scan Document.
  2. Tap Scan Documents. The first time, allow MarkPDF to use the camera.
  3. Hold your iPhone over the page. With Auto on, the page is captured as soon as its edges are found. Tap the shutter button to capture it yourself.
  4. Scan the next page, and the next. The thumbnail in the corner shows your last scan, and the Save button counts the pages.
  5. Tap Save. Your pages are saved together as one PDF.

Tips for a clean scan

  • Wipe the camera lens first. A smudge blurs every page.
  • Scan in good, even light, and use the flash button when the room is dim.
  • Lay the page flat on a darker surface, so its edges stand out.

MarkPDF is free on the App Store for iPhone.
