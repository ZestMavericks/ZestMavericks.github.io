How to summarise a PDF with AI on iPhone

Updated 1 October 2026

Open MarkPDF, tap Summarise PDF, then Choose a File and pick the document. MarkPDF gives you a short summary, so you can see what a long PDF covers before you read all of it.

Step by step

  1. Open MarkPDF and tap Summarise PDF on the home screen.
  2. Tap Choose a File and pick the PDF.
  3. Wait a moment while the summary is written.
  4. Read the summary, then open the parts of the document that need your full attention.

What happens to your document

To write the summary, the text of that one document is sent to our AI provider, and it isn't kept afterwards. Everything else you do in MarkPDF stays on your iPhone. The details are in the privacy policy.

AI summaries are part of MarkPDF Pro. See the plans and prices.

MarkPDF is free on the App Store for iPhone.
