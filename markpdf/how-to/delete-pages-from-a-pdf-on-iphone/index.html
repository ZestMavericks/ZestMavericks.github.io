How to delete pages from a PDF on iPhone

Updated 1 October 2026

In MarkPDF, tap Delete Pages, choose the PDF, then tap each page you want to remove so it's ticked, and tap Delete. Tap Preview first to check the document without those pages.

Step by step

  1. Open MarkPDF. On the home screen, under Edit, tap Delete Pages.
  2. Choose the PDF you want to change.
  3. Tap each page you want to remove. A tick marks it, and the bar at the bottom shows how many you've selected. Tap Deselect All to start over.
  4. Tap Preview to see the document without the selected pages.
  5. Tap Delete.

Good to know

  • Every page has its number under the thumbnail, so you can match it to the page you saw while reading.
  • To keep only a few pages, tick the ones you don't need. There's no limit to how many you select.
  • Deleting pages is part of MarkPDF Pro.

MarkPDF is free on the App Store for iPhone.
