Guides

Get it done in a minute

Step by step answers for the things people do most with PDFs on an iPhone.

  • How to delete pages from a PDF on iPhone In MarkPDF, tap Delete Pages, choose the PDF, then tap each page you want to remove so it's ticked, and tap Delete. Tap Preview first to check the document without those pages.
  • How to password-protect a PDF on iPhone Open MarkPDF, tap Lock PDF, choose the document, type a password and tap Lock PDF. The PDF then asks for that password before it opens, and Remove Lock takes the password off again.
  • How to scan documents to PDF on iPhone Open MarkPDF, tap Scan Document, then Scan Documents, and hold your iPhone over the page. MarkPDF finds the edges of the page and captures it; scan every page, tap Save, and they're saved together as a PDF.
  • How to summarise a PDF with AI on iPhone Open MarkPDF, tap Summarise PDF, then Choose a File and pick the document. MarkPDF gives you a short summary, so you can see what a long PDF covers before you read all of it.
