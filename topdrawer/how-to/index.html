TopDrawer guides

Short answers for keeping and finding the things you save on your iPhone.

  • How to lock saved passwords and notes with Face ID on iPhone Save them in TopDrawer and turn on its lock: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is then needed before TopDrawer opens. TopDrawer offers the lock the first time you save something that looks like a password, and it falls back to your passcode, so you can't lock yourself out.
  • How to search for text inside screenshots on iPhone Save the screenshot to TopDrawer, then type any word you remember from it into Search everything. TopDrawer reads the text in every screenshot on your iPhone, so the one with that word comes up, even if you never gave it a name.
