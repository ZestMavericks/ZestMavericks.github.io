How to lock saved passwords and notes with Face ID on iPhone

Updated 1 October 2026

Save them in TopDrawer and turn on its lock: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is then needed before TopDrawer opens. TopDrawer offers the lock the first time you save something that looks like a password, and it falls back to your passcode, so you can't lock yourself out.

Step by step

  1. Save the password, note or screenshot in TopDrawer.
  2. When TopDrawer offers to lock the app, turn the lock on. If you skipped it, you can switch it on later in TopDrawer's settings.
  3. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  4. From then on, TopDrawer asks for Face ID before it opens, and hides its contents in the app switcher while it's locked.

Good to know

  • Once unlocked, TopDrawer stays open for a minute, so copying a password doesn't ask twice.
  • TopDrawer only learns yes or no from iOS. Your face or fingerprint data never leaves your iPhone's Secure Enclave.
