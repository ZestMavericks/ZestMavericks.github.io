TopDrawer

One drawer for everything you save. Put a screenshot, a link, a PDF or a stray password in it, and find any of them again with one search.

See what Ultra adds

Coming to iPhone. Requires iOS 26 or later.

The idea

The things you save are never in one place

A screenshot of a Wi-Fi card goes to Photos. A link goes to a reading list you never open. A booking reference ends up in a note, or a message to yourself. When you need one of them, you go looking in four apps.

  • Text, links, screenshots, images, PDFs and files
  • Share to it from any app, or paste straight in
  • Everything in one grid, newest and most used first

Search

It reads your screenshots, so you don't have to

Every image and document you save is read on your iPhone, and the words inside it become searchable. The Wi-Fi password in that photo is findable by typing the password, or the hotel, or the word "network".

  • Results as you type, from the first few letters
  • Searches titles, notes, tags and recognised text together
  • Text recognition happens on the device, not on a server

Previews

Every kind of thing looks like itself

A wall of identical grey tiles is something you have to read. TopDrawer shows each item as what it actually is, so you can scan the drawer instead.

Notes show their words

Not a file name and an icon. The first lines of what you saved, set the way it deserves.

Code looks like code

A command, a JSON blob or a snippet is set in a monospaced face, because alignment is half the meaning.

A colour is the colour

Paste a hex value and you get the swatch, not six grey characters you have to imagine.

Links know where they go

The site, the path and a mark that stays the same every time, so you recognise it before you read it.

Documents open properly

A PDF is a PDF you can read and scroll, not a picture of its first page.

Pictures fill the frame

Full resolution, pinch to zoom, and one tap to copy whatever text was in them.

Everywhere else

You should not have to open it to use it

The fastest way to find something is the way that takes the fewest taps. Often that means not launching an app at all.

iPhone search

Saved items appear in Spotlight, including by the text recognised inside them. One switch turns it off, and turning it off takes them back out again.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask Siri to search TopDrawer, or open a saved item by name. Saving is a Shortcuts action, so anything you can automate can end up in the drawer.

The share sheet

Share to TopDrawer from any app. It copies what you sent and gets out of the way, so it never stalls the app you were in.

Lock

Face ID, offered when it starts to matter

An unlocked phone handed over for a photo should not also hand over the passwords you saved. TopDrawer can sit behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode — and it asks the first time you save something that looks like a secret, rather than on the day you install it.

  • Hidden in the app switcher while it is locked
  • Stays open for a minute, so copying a password doesn't ask twice
  • Falls back to your passcode, so you are never locked out

Privacy

There is no server to send it to

This is not a setting you have to find. TopDrawer has no account, no sync and nowhere to upload anything, because it was built without one.

Nothing is uploaded

What you save stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS and your passcode.

The reading happens here

Text recognition and tagging run on the device, using what Apple already ships. No document is sent anywhere to be processed.

Nobody is counting

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across other apps or sites. We do not know what you save.

Questions

Before you ask

Is TopDrawer free?
Yes. The free drawer holds 30 items, and every feature works in it. Ultra removes the limit, monthly, yearly or once for life: the plans are below.
Does TopDrawer upload what I save?
No. There's no account, no sync and no server of ours to send it to. Everything stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone. The only connection the app makes is to our payments provider, to check whether you've bought Ultra.
Can it find words inside my screenshots?
Yes. TopDrawer reads the text in every image and document you save, on your iPhone, using Apple's own text recognition. Type a word you remember from a screenshot and it comes up.
Which iPhones does it work on?
Any iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
Does it sync to my iPad or Mac?
No. Your library stays on your iPhone. It's included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple encrypts and we can't see.
Can I lock it?
Yes, with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. TopDrawer offers the lock the first time you save something that looks like a password, and it falls back to your passcode, so you're never locked out.
Does it read my clipboard?
Only when you tap Paste. Never when it opens, and never in the background.
When can I get it?
