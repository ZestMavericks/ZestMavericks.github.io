2. What the App does

TopDrawer is a private vault for the things you want to find again. It can:

  • Save text, links, images, screenshots, PDFs and other files
  • Accept them from the share sheet, from the clipboard, or from Shortcuts
  • Read the text inside images and documents on your device, so it becomes searchable
  • Suggest a title and tags for what you saved, on your device
  • Search everything you have saved, including that recognised text
  • Publish items to iPhone search (Spotlight), if you leave that switched on
  • Answer Siri requests and provide Shortcuts actions
  • Sit behind Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, if you switch that on

Features vary by device and by iOS version. On-device title and tag suggestions use Apple Intelligence where the device supports it, and fall back to a simpler method where it does not. The App works either way.

4. What TopDrawer is not

This matters more than the usual disclaimer, because people keep useful things in a drawer.

  • It is not an encrypted password manager. What you save is protected by iOS file protection, your device passcode, and the App's optional lock. That is meaningful protection, and it is not the same as a dedicated password manager with its own encryption and master password. Use one of those for credentials that really matter.

5. The free drawer and Ultra

TopDrawer is free to download. A free drawer holds up to 30 saved items, and every feature works within it. Once it is full, the App stops accepting new saves until you remove something or upgrade; nothing you already saved is removed, hidden or held hostage.

TopDrawer Ultra removes the item limit. If you let an Ultra subscription lapse while holding more than 30 items, your items stay exactly where they are and remain searchable; you simply cannot add new ones until you are back under the limit or subscribed again.
